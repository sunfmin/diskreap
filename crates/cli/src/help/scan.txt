diskreap scan — find large directories no Rule covers, deleting nothing.

WHEN   To find what to write the next Rule for.
WHO    Anyone. What it finds becomes a Rule only after a person agrees.
READS  Everything under ~ (not looking into what a Rule covers, nor other
       volumes), $TMPDIR, ~/Library/Application Support, the apps installed
       and the programs running. WRITES nothing. Walking ~ can take minutes.

ANSWERS
  gist       the gist's id
  large      up to 30 directories of 1 GiB or more with no child that large,
             largest first: path, bytes
  tmpdir     $TMPDIR's entries grouped by what made them (bk-catalog-10096-0
             and bk-catalog-11062-12 are both bk-catalog), largest first:
             group, glob, entries, bytes
  orphans    Application Support directories of 10 MiB or more that no
             installed app's name or bundle id resembles: path, bytes, why,
             and rule, a draft to complete and check
  seconds    how long it took
  next       what to do with the findings

EVENTS (stderr) {"event":"large"|"tmpdir"|"orphan",...} as each is found,
and {"event":"start","scan":...} as each part starts.

TRIPS YOU UP
  It guesses: a large directory may be your work, and an orphan may belong
  to an app it does not know. Ask the person before any becomes a Rule.
